# Adjectives

An adjective can stand in one of two positions:

1. It can be an <span style="color:red">**attributive adjective**</span>, i.e. the adjective modifies the following
   noun:

> **een groene auto**

2. It can be used independently, i.e. not modifying a noun (it is not modified):

> **de auto is groen**

> **hij zingt mooi**

## The final `-e`

The adjective always takes the final e

> **De groen<span style="color:red">**e**</span> auto staat in de still<span style="color:red">**e**</span> straat**

> **Een groen<span style="color:red">**e**</span> auto staat in een still<span style="color:red">**e**</span> straat.**

> **Het groen<span style="color:red">**e**</span> gras groeit op het grot<span style="color:red">**e**</span> plein.**

## Exceptions

### Attributive adjectives without `-e` ending

These include adjectives ending in `-en` such as **gouden**, **zilveren**, **katoenen**,
**wollen**, **stoffen** etc.

These adjectives usually describe materials from which objects are made or
are past participles ending in `-en` used as adjectives (gebakken eieren):


> **Dat is een katoenen bloes.**

> **Dat is een mooie, zilveren ring.**

> **Ik houd van leren schoenen.** _I love leather shoes._

### `iets`/`wat`/`niets` + adjective + `-s`

> **Hij ziet <span style="color:red">**iets**</span> mooi<span style="color:red">**s**</span> in elke situatie.** _He
sees something good in every situation._

> **Ik wil je <span style="color:red">**wat**</span> lekker<span style="color:red">**s**</span> geven.** _I want to give
you something tasty._

> Zij willen <span style="color:red">**niets**</span> negatief<span style="color:red">**s**</span> horen over hun
> kinderen.

## Adverbs

Adverbs that modify verbs, adjectives or other adverbs have the same form as the uninflected adjective, as they often do
in colloquial English:


> **Het zijn <span style="color:red">**aardige**</span> mensen**. _They are nice people_.

> **Zij zingt <span style="color:red">**aardig**</span>.** _She sings nicely._

> **Hij schrijft <span style="color:red">**goede**</span> brieven**. _He writes good letters_.

> **Hij schrijft <span style="color:red">**goed**</span>.** _He writes well_.
